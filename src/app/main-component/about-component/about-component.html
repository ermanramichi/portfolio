Get to know me

I'm Erman — a 23-year-old developer from Skopje, in my final year of Software Engineering at SS. Cyril and Methodius University.

I care about the details most people scroll past: how a layout holds up on a small screen, how fast a page feels on a slow connection, how obvious the next click is. Most of my time goes into front-end work with Angular, TypeScript and Tailwind, backed by Java on the server side.

I believe in giving my all to master new skills and perfect my craft — and I'm ready for whatever challenge comes next.
